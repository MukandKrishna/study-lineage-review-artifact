# Paper evaluation scope

The primary comparison includes 14 clinical families:

- CF-P1--CF-P3: provenance;
- CF-A1--CF-A3: applicability;
- CF-D1--CF-D3: result direction;
- H3: held-out composition;
- P5R, P6R, H5, and H6: later study-grouping and composition tests.

This gives 110 exact inputs and 550 calls per model, or 2,750 calls across five models. Repeated calls measure stability; the clinical family is the experimental unit.

Oracle tests, cue ablations, prompt-order checks, and the lineage-overlap control are reported separately and are not part of this denominator.
